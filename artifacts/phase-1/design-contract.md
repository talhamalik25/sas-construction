# Industrial Editorial hero contract

Surface: SAS Construction homepage hero, retaining the existing navbar.
Audience: clients assessing an independent contractor's experience and practical site execution.
Primary action: Explore Our Projects; secondary: Get in Touch. Future destinations open an honest, dismissible coming-soon dialog.
Direction: oversized two-line editorial title over a panoramic earthworks photograph, with a horizontal description/action rail. This replaces the former side-by-side template. Sand-gold rules and one fine registration mark provide restrained construction-drawing references.
Hierarchy: brand/navigation, contractor/experience eyebrow, headline, scope and actions, immersive representative photo with clear provenance.
Typography: local Manrope variable headings and Inter variable body. Brand colors unchanged.
Evidence: project brief requires hands-on contracting; design system prefers editorial rhythm, actual-looking earthworks photography, and no unsupported credentials. Client explicitly requested full-width industrial editorial direction.
Viewports: 1440x900, 1366x768, 960x900, 768x1024, 390x844, 320x740.
States: local photo/font loading reserves geometry; partial pages use coming-soon dialogs; no forms, authentication, asynchronous data, or success claims. Failed media must preserve descriptive alt text and background; primary text and actions remain available without JavaScript.
Motion: GSAP entrance translation and a subtle image scale reveal when the image enters view, scoped and cleaned up on unmount. Reduced motion renders final positions immediately. No pinning, scrub, parallax, or hidden content dependency.
