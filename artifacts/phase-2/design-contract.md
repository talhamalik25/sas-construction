# About & Experience design contract

Scope: one section directly after the approved hero. Header, hero, logo, global styles and shared typography must remain byte-for-byte unchanged.
Audience: potential construction clients evaluating experience and hands-on execution.
Direction: a typographic 20+ occupies the main field, paired with a smaller offset portrait photograph; the supplied headline sits beneath the numeral, followed by an asymmetric capability list and description. No generic cards, progress indicators or invented claims.
Content: exact user-supplied heading, description and three capability labels. One h2, no additional h1.
Photo: existing licensed Defrino Maasy image, clearly labelled representative with credit; no claim it depicts SAS work.
Viewports: 1440x900, 1366x768, 768x1024, 390x844, 320x740.
Interactions: a real photographer-credit link; no new CTA or form. Scoped GSAP translation reveals on scroll, no opacity hiding, counter animation, parallax or pinning. Reduced-motion preferences remove transforms and triggers.
States: static server-rendered content; image loading reserves dimensions, failed imagery leaves descriptive alt text and the text section usable. Empty/success/permission states do not apply; no remote data, submission, authentication or permissions. Partial information is explicitly handled through the representative-image disclosure.
Acceptance: no horizontal overflow, text and image load, no browser errors, zero axe violations, correct heading/capabilities, no prohibited copy; keyboard focus and photo credit verified. Approved source hashes must match.
