## Website objective
Help people assess contractor experience, understand services, review known project scopes, and initiate a direct enquiry. Keep the writing practical, straightforward, respectful and specific: show real construction execution rather than aspirational property development marketing.

## Primary audiences
- Housing societies and developers requiring road and infrastructure contractors
- General contractors looking for capable subcontractors
- Property/site owners requiring earthwork and site development
- Organisations procuring road, drainage, utility, and land preparation work

## Proposed pages
- `/` — Home
- `/about` — About
- `/services` — Services
- `/projects` — Projects
- `/contact` — Contact

## In scope
Brand-consistent responsive website, services and project listings, accessible navigation, SEO basics, enquiry CTA, editable project data, clean visual presentation, image usage disclosure where needed.

## Out of scope unless requested
Booking system, customer dashboard, payments, CMS, fabricated testimonials, dynamic project status tracking, speculative partner logos, quotations shown as fixed prices.

## Unknown information to request from client
- Owner / authorized contact name and preferred title
- Phone, WhatsApp, email and office/service address
- Finalized spellings, cities and location descriptions for all nine projects
- Whether each was direct work, subcontracting, or labour/equipment contribution
- Actual worksite photos and permission to publish
- Public service area, preferred enquiry route and domain
- Which services are currently offered and if any need removing

## Decisions that should not block initial UI
Use internal data placeholders clearly marked `TODO: client detail`. Hide unverified contact fields in the public-facing UI rather than publishing placeholders. The first deliverable needs no backend.
