# SAS Construction — Agent Handoff

**Status:** Pre-development documentation, version 1.0 (14 November 2026)

## Mission
Build a premium, credible, light-themed website for **SAS Construction**, a Karachi-area independent construction contractor. It is a hands-on contractor business, **not** a large real-estate developer or a multinational organization. The owner has stated **20+ years of field experience** and personally oversees machinery, labour, site activities, and execution.

## Read in order
1. `01_PROJECT_BRIEF.md` — real business context, scope, constraints.
2. `02_BRAND_DESIGN_SYSTEM.md` — approved visual direction, identity, UI specifications.
3. `03_CONTENT_AND_SITEMAP.md` — page sections and approved draft copy.
4. `04_PROJECTS_DATA.md` — nine supplied project records and open questions.
5. `05_AGENT_BUILD_RULES.md` — execution workflow and acceptance tests.
6. `assets/sas-logo-reference.png` — approved logo concept/image; use as supplied, do not redraw.

## Critical guardrails
- **Use exactly “SAS Construction” as brand name.** Do not add a business classification as a subtitle in the logo.
- Do not introduce the previously rejected two-word industry label anywhere in user-visible copy, metadata, asset names, or UI.
- Do not invent achievements, machinery ownership counts, contract values, team sizes, licenses, client endorsements, dates, or contacts.
- The names NLC and FWO are **project labels supplied by the client**, not proof of a direct contractual relationship.
- Generic/licensed imagery must be clearly distinguished from documentary photos of the listed sites.
- Missing contact details and unclear project spellings remain **unverified** until the client confirms them.

## Suggested initial milestone
Only implement the design foundation, navigation, footer, and homepage hero in the first delivery. Send a screenshot and file-change report for approval before implementing more sections.
